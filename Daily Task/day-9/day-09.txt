Generics basics; bounded types; wildcards (PECS); try-with-resources; AutoCloseable

   copyEntities(List<? extends BaseEntity> src, List<? super BaseEntity> dest)
6. try-with-resources CSV data loading (lost_items.csv, found_items.csv, users.csv)
7. Custom AutoCloseable (LockerAuditSession) demonstrating exact LIFO close order
